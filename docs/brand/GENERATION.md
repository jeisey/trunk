## Horizontal logo

Use case: logo-brand.
Create ONE final production logo asset for "trunk", a mobile-first activity and day-planning tool for parents of babies and preschoolers. This is the actual transparent logo, not a presentation, mockup, contact sheet, or brand board.
Composition: a wide horizontal lockup on a transparent canvas, desired dimensions 1536 x 512 px, aspect ratio 3:1. A small distinctive elephant-head symbol at left, and the exact lowercase word "trunk" at right. Fill the width comfortably with even modest transparent padding; do not surround a tiny logo with a huge blank canvas. Vertically align the mark and lettering optically. The mark should remain recognizable at 32 px tall.
Elephant symbol: a single simple rounded elephant head in side profile, a bold gently curled upward trunk, one large round ear, one small friendly eye. No tusks, no body or legs, no additional characters. Clever confident silhouette with sturdy geometry and open negative space. Gentle anthropomorphic warmth without looking like a toy store or daycare cartoon.
Typography: exact text "trunk", t-r-u-n-k, lowercase only. Custom-looking geometric humanist sans serif, bold enough for a small website header, generous x-height, friendly softly rounded terminals, careful kerning, highly legible. No slogan or other text.
Color palette: deep forest green #163E35 for the wordmark and elephant silhouette, muted sky blue #6BABC4 for the elephant's large ear. Two solid flat colors only, with antialiasing at edges. The little eye may be a clean transparent knockout.
Style: exceptionally clean, minimal, crisp, vector-like flat logo design, tasteful editorial polish, calm practical contemporary parenting brand. All shapes opaque except true negative spaces and the background.
Background: genuinely fully transparent alpha, including the space inside letters. Absolutely no white rectangle, no fake checkerboard, no shadow, no gradients, no texture, no 3D, no glow, no border, no watermark. Do not show any dimensions, palette swatches, labels, or usage guidelines in the image.
